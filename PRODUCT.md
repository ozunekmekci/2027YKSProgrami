# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Node.js with ExcelJS to generate a modern, highly-polished, multi-sheet XLSX workbook with dynamic dependent dropdowns, exact formulas, formatting, and hyperlinks.

## Users

Primary User: High-school student / graduate preparing for YKS (TYT and AYT) with an Equal Weight (EA) and Verbal (Sözel) target.
Characteristics:
- Starts from absolute zero ("hiçbir şey bilmeyen").
- Dislikes heavy studying; prone to burnout if overwhelmed.
- Needs mandatory, non-negotiable break intervals.
- Thrives on clear, frictionless, pre-structured schedules where they never have to guess "which video was I on?".

## Product Purpose

An interactive, automated Excel workbook and study planner that structures 746 YouTube videos (~444 hours across 9 essential YKS playlists) into a structured daily routine. Guarantees that the student finishes all curriculum videos before the deadline of 19 June 2027 without cognitive fatigue.

## Positioning

Unlike generic static study schedules or chaotic checklists, this tool:
1. Embeds exact YouTube playlist metadata (video titles, precise durations in minutes, real instructors, and direct click-to-watch links).
2. Provides a zero-decision daily rhythm: 4 blocks per day, 1 video per block, 20-minute breaks between blocks.
3. Features an interactive planner with dependent dropdowns (pick Subject -> see only its videos -> duration auto-populates).
4. Strictly enforces "Sunday is No Study Day" (ÇALIŞMAK YASAK) to prevent student burnout.

## Operating Context

The student opens the Excel spreadsheet daily on PC, laptop, or tablet. They mark videos as watched (`[X]`), view live progress bars, click links to watch directly, and know exactly what 4 blocks to complete each day.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Strict Daily Routine**: 4 blocks/day, 1 block = 1 video.
- **Mandatory Breaks**: 20 minutes between blocks.
- **Strict Video Chronology**: Videos progress sequentially (#1, #2, #3...) without breaking order.
- **Deadline**: 19 June 2027.
- **Sundays**: Strictly off (Rest day).
- **Excel Capabilities**:
  - Dependent data validation dropdowns (Ders -> Video Başlığı).
  - Instant duration lookup (`XLOOKUP` / `VLOOKUP`).
  - Total daily/weekly study time and break time counters.
  - Formatted click-to-play YouTube hyperlinks.
  - Visual completion indicators (conditional formatting green when completed).

## Brand Commitments

- Straightforward, encouraging, clean, non-intimidating design.
- Clear visual cues: gentle pastel tones, high readability, no overwhelming dense walls of text.

## Product Principles

1. **Zero Cognitive Friction**: The student never has to ask "what should I study today?". The next video is already queued.
2. **Burnout Defense**: Fixed 4 blocks and strict 20-minute breaks make each day bite-sized and non-threatening.
3. **Evidence-Based Durations**: Exact real-world video minutes instead of vague "1 hour study" approximations.
4. **Guaranteed Completion**: Math-backed scheduling ensures everything finishes before 19 June 2027.
